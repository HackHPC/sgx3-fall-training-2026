**Presenters:** Je'aime Powell (HPC Specialist & Workforce Development Lead), Dr. Linda Hayden (Co-PI & Workforce Development Lead)

## Structuring the 40-Hour Cyberinfrastructure Roadmap

On October 21, 2026, the SGX3 Fall Training cohort gathered for Session 2, pivoting from general orientation to technical execution and program logistics. Lead instructor Je'aime Powell and Co-PI Dr. Linda Hayden introduced the official **2026 Fall Training Task Sheet**, a comprehensive 40-hour professional development roadmap that trainees must complete on or before Wednesday, December 30th, to receive their $500 financial stipend.

The task sheet establishes rigorous benchmarks across foundational tools and national platforms. Technical milestones include completing industry certifications in **Vim Essentials** (command-line text editing for headless HPC environments), **DataCamp Introduction to Git**, **Kaggle Python Certification**, **Bandit OverTheWire Linux Wargames**, **Spring Boot for Beginners** (building CRUD APIs with Postgres), **Kaggle Introduction to Machine Learning**, and **Google Cloud Computing Foundations**. Professional milestones mandate updating Slack profiles, optimizing LinkedIn accounts, reviewing Science Gateways community webinars, applying to at least two summer research internships or conference programs (such as PEARC, SC, or ORNL PCIP), and establishing active accounts on national HPC, AI, and Super Intelligence (SI) infrastructure—including ACCESS-CI, NAIRR, CloudBank, and the National Research Platform (NRP)—accompanied by a written reflection detailing how these computational resources support their coursework and scientific inquiry.

## Empowering Student Leaders Through Peer Mentorship

To foster a collaborative research environment, Session 2 launched a peer mentorship model wherein returning student veterans co-lead training modules. Experienced scholars were assigned specific leadership roles based on prior certifications:

- **Qimora Mason** was designated to lead the Kaggle Python Certification walkthroughs.
- **Abraham Ashade** and **Dillon "Lo" Moore** were selected to guide the cohort through the terminal mechanics of the Bandit wargames.
- **Kayden** was assigned to facilitate the Vim Essentials command-line session.
- **Suniyah Esey** was appointed to lead the Kaggle Introduction to Machine Learning module.

Instructor Powell emphasized that while returning scholars lead specific sessions, the entire cohort is encouraged to utilize Slack and shared Zoom rooms to collaborate and troubleshoot complex assignments collectively.

## Strategic Logistics and AI-Driven Web Development

The session outlined upcoming semester constraints, aligning training deadlines around university final exams and the Supercomputing conference (SC26), with final GitHub Pages portfolio presentations scheduled for late November and mid-December.

Closing the session, Je'aime Powell conducted a live demonstration showing how to rapidly prototype and deploy a public GitHub Pages portfolio using modern AI models (such as Z.AI / GLM 5.2 and Claude). Powell demonstrated proper role-prompting techniques ("You are a web developer specializing in professional portfolios on GitHub Pages"), fed context from exported LinkedIn profiles to populate resume data, enabled GitHub Pages deployment from the repository's `main` branch, and stressed the importance of maintaining a project context log file (`README.md` / `pick_up_and_go.md`) to preserve project state and conserve computational tokens across AI sessions.
